The 2026 survey collected 9,400 responses from professional developers. Seventy-one percent use an AI coding assistant at least weekly, up from 52% in 2025.

Among weekly users, 58% say the assistant's biggest benefit is understanding unfamiliar code, ahead of writing new code (41%) and generating tests (33%).

Trust remains conditional. Only 29% of respondents accept assistant suggestions without reading them, and 63% want assistants to show the sources behind their suggestions.

Developers at companies with formal AI usage policies were 1.6 times more likely to use assistants daily, suggesting that clear rules increase rather than reduce adoption.

Spending intent is strongest for tools that integrate with existing review workflows: 47% of team leads plan to increase budgets for review-integrated assistants in 2027.
